# Research Methodology

The research process combined:

* Company websites
* LinkedIn
* Tofler & MCA filings
* Industry directories
* Trade expo catalogs
* Job postings
* News and expansion announcements

Each company was manually reviewed before final scoring.

---

# Evaluation Framework

Companies were evaluated using DeepThought’s Federer framework:

* **C3** → Differentiation
* **C4** → Decision-Maker Quality
* **C5** → Sector Tailwinds
* **C6** → Growth Signals
* **C7** → Systems Maturity
* **C8** → Leadership Succession

Additional eligibility gates:

* E1 → Producer Check
* E2 → Operational Accessibility in India

---

# Important Note

AI tools were used as research assistants for structuring and accelerating workflows. However:

* Company verification was manually reviewed
* Scores were manually adjusted
* Rejected companies were independently filtered
* Final judgment and qualification logic were human-reviewed

---

# Key Learnings

* Systems maturity strongly correlates with scaling capability
* ERP/SAP adoption is a strong operational maturity indicator
* Many specialty manufacturers fail due to weak succession planning
* Growth signals become clearer through hiring and expansion activity
